1. Executive System Overview

Eitekh WorkOS is an all-in-one work management and issue tracking operating system engineered for enterprise agility, compliance, and large-scale IT/ERP transformations. It combines modern agile execution frameworks (Scrum, Kanban, Gantt Roadmaps) with native SAP Activate Methodology Governance, strict Policy-Based Access Control (PBAC), and real-time Server-Sent Events (SSE) data synchronization.

3. Complete Functional Modules

A. Organization & Tenant Governance

  • Multi-Organization Architecture: Single user accounts can span multiple isolated organizations with secure context switching.
  • Workspaces & Teams: Granular grouping of departments, initiatives, and teams with role assignments.
  • Tokenized Member Invitations: Secure invitation workflow with cryptographic tokens, automated 7-day expiration, and pre-assigned RBAC roles.

B. Agile Workspace Views (Interactive Suite)

  • Kanban Board: Customizable status columns, WIP limit thresholds with visual overload warnings, and drag-and-drop cards.
  • Interactive List View: Dense data grid with multi-selection checkboxes and a floating Bulk Actions Bar for batch status, priority, or deletion.
  • Scrum & Backlog: Backlog triage, sprint lifecycle controls (Plan, Start, Complete), velocity points, and sprint metrics.
  • Gantt / Timeline Roadmap: Real date-span bars, priority coloring, dynamic calendar zoom, and interactive dependency arrows.
  • Calendar View: Monthly milestone scheduler with due-date badges and instant issue preview drawer.
  • Team Workload & Capacity: Real-time bandwidth utilization calculations, over-allocation alerts (e.g. 120% capacity), and balanced workload distribution.

C. Native SAP Activate Implementation Methodology

A premier enterprise differentiator built natively into Eitekh WorkOS for SAP ERP and S/4HANA digital transformations:

  • 6 Activate Phase Gates: Discover → Prepare → Explore → Realize → Deploy → Run.
  • Fit-to-Standard Workshops: Interactive workshop evaluator with 6 decision outcomes (Adopt, Configure, Extend, Integrate, Defer, Out of Scope).
  • Automated Backlog Generator: Automatically converts "Extend" and "Integrate" decisions into WRICEF development and configuration tickets with full requirement-to-code traceability.
  • Quality Gate Governance (Q-Gates): Formal phase-exit sign-off workflow enforcing strict separation of duties (4-eyes principle between Gate Raiser and Gate Approver).
  • Deliverable Worksheets & Workstreams: Pre-built tracking across Project Management, Application Design, Integration, Testing, Data Migration, Technical Architecture, and OCM.

D. Executive Analytics & Reporting Center

  • 10 Enterprise Deep-Dive Reports: Sprint Velocity, Burndown/Burnup, Cumulative Flow Diagram (CFD), Lead & Cycle Time, Defect Leakage, Epic Progress, Workload Allocation, Issue Stagnation, Traceability Matrix, and Project SLA Compliance.
  • Multi-Format Instant Export: PDF Document (Multi-Page Print-Ready) Microsoft Excel (.xlsx Raw Sheets) Standard CSV (Warehouse-Ready)
